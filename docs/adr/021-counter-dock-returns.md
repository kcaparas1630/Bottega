# ADR 021 — The add dock is the shop counter again

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Amends:** [ADR 019](019-storefront-atmosphere-plain-controls.md) and
  [ADR 020](020-commercial-heritage-shopfront.md) — the one exception to "controls stay plain"

## Context

The plain line-icon dock was correct but forgettable. The counter dock of
[ADR 017](017-storefront-character-with-a-serious-finish.md) — a dark counter with small drawn goods
for photos, text and shapes — was the element people remembered, and it is still the right
signature: it sits low on the screen, holds only three items, and says what each one adds at a
glance.

## Decision

- The add dock is the counter from ADR 017: Brick Ink, a thin clay lip on top, and three upright
  goods drawn with plain boxes — two photo prints, two letter tiles, three paper shapes — over
  small-caps labels. Goods rise slightly on hover; the open one's label turns mustard.
- It is the only control drawn as an object. Everything else stays plain, as ADRs 019 and 020 say.
- The pickers that open above the counter keep the current quiet style.

## Consequences

- One memorable, ownable element in the chrome, balanced by the flat fascia and plain panels.
- Any further control-as-object needs its own ADR.
